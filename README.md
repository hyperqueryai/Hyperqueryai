### 💼 Flagship Proof-of-Work Projects

#### 1. [B2B SaaS Net Revenue Retention & Cohort Churn Engine](https://github.com/hyperqueryai/saas-churn-cohort-retention)
* **The Problem:** Pinpointing activation drop-off points driving monthly gross logo churn in a recurring subscription platform.
* **Key Delivery:** Modular SQL pipeline calculating Logo Retention % and Net Revenue Retention (NRR) across monthly user cohorts.
* **Tech Stack:** SQL (Window Functions, CTEs), DuckDB, Streamlit Cloud.
* **Artifacts:** Interactive Dashboard · Functional Requirements Document (FRD)

#### 2. [European FinTech SEPA Anomaly & AML Monitoring](https://github.com/hyperqueryai/fintech-aml-sepa-monitoring)
* **The Problem:** Detecting structuring ("smurfing") and velocity anomalies in SEPA payment streams under EU AML directives.
* **Key Delivery:** Automated ingestion via European Central Bank REST API, multi-currency normalization, and rolling-window transaction risk scoring.
* **Tech Stack:** Python (Requests, Pandas), PostgreSQL, Power BI.
* **Artifacts:** Live Executive Dashboard · Risk Compliance Audit Memo
